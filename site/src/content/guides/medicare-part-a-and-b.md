## Medicare Part A and Part B: Full Onboarding

<p>This guide walks through applying online for Medicare Part A and Part B through the Social Security Administration (SSA). It is an educational walkthrough to help you prepare for your Exact Benefits consultation; SSA makes eligibility and enrollment decisions.</p>

## Before you begin

<ul> <li>If you are working past 65 or have employer group health coverage, confirm your Part B timing with your employer benefits team or SSA before submitting.</li> <li>Keep your Social Security number, place of birth, current mailing address, and phone number ready.</li> <li>Do not send Social Security or Medicare numbers by email.</li> </ul>

## Step 1: Open SSA.gov and choose Medicare

<p>From the SSA homepage, open the Medicare menu.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 1](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-01.webp)

## Step 2: Choose Sign up for Medicare

<p>Select the enrollment option and choose Apply for myself.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 2](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-02.webp)

## Step 3: Choose how to sign in

<p>Use a secure sign-in option already associated with your account.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 3](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-03.webp)

## Step 4: Sign in with Login.gov or create an account

<p>Enter your credentials only on the official sign-in page.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 4](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-04.webp)

## Step 5: Continue through the secure sign-in flow

<p>Read and accept the required terms, then continue.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 5](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-05.webp)

## Step 6: Review the application information

<p>Review the eligibility and application information before starting.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 6](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-06.webp)

## Step 7: Begin the Medicare application

<p>The application explains Part A and the Part B decision.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 7](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-07.webp)

## Step 8: Confirm your address type

<p>Answer whether your mailing or home address is outside the U.S.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 8](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-08.webp)

## Step 9: Select language support preferences

<p>Choose the preferred language for speaking and reading.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 9](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-09.webp)

## Step 10: Confirm your identity details

<p>Confirm the information SSA has on file.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 10](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-10.webp)

## Step 11: Enter your mailing address

<p>Enter your current mailing address and indicate whether you live there.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 11](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-11.webp)

## Step 12: Verify the mailing address

<p>Choose the USPS-standard address if it is correct.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 12](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-12.webp)

## Step 13: Provide your contact information

<p>Enter a phone number SSA can use to contact you.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 13](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-13.webp)

## Step 14: Choose Medicare Part A and Part B

<p>Select whether you are applying for Part B as well as Part A.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 14](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-14.webp)

## Step 15: Answer the Medicaid question

<p>Answer the Medicaid question accurately.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 15](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-15.webp)

## Step 16: Answer the OPM annuity question

<p>Answer the retirement-benefit question accurately.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 16](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-16.webp)

## Step 17: Review your application

<p>Check every section and edit anything that is not correct.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 17](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-17.webp)

## Step 18: Accept the electronic-signature agreement and submit

<p>Agree to the electronic signature only after the application is complete, then select Submit now.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 18](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-18.webp)

## Step 19: Confirm your application was submitted

<p>Confirm that Part A and Part B are listed and the application was submitted.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 19](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-19.webp)

## Step 20: Save your application receipt

<p>Open and keep a copy of the application receipt for your records.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 20](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-20.webp)

## Step 21: Return to your my Social Security account

<p>Return to your account to follow the application.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 21](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-21.webp)

## Step 22: Monitor the application review status

<p>Check the current review stage in Your Benefit Applications.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 22](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-22.webp)

## Step 23: Review the status details

<p>Use the status details to understand what is underway.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 23](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-23.webp)

## Step 24: Open your Benefit Verification Letter

<p>Open Your Benefit Verification Letter when it becomes available.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 24](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-24.webp)

## Step 25: Download the Benefit Verification Letter

<p>Download the letter using a secure device and keep it private.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 25](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-25.webp)

## Step 26: Confirm your Medicare effective dates and number

<p>Review the Medicare information section for your effective dates and Medicare number.</p>

![Medicare enrollment walkthrough — Step 26](../../assets/images/guides/guide-medicare-part-a-and-b-step-26.webp)
